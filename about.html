Who We Are

AEGIS IoT Technologies is a smart home hardware and consumer electronics company established to make modern home automation truly universal, accessible, and privacy-respecting.

Traditional smart home ecosystems force consumers into walled gardens, demanding expensive proprietary gateway hubs, complex setup procedures, and monthly recurring cloud subscription fees for basic functions like camera recording or remote fan control. We believe smart living should simply work over your standard Wi-Fi router.

Our Core Product Portfolio

We design and manufacture high-reliability, generic Wi-Fi-enabled home tech that integrates seamlessly into everyday living:

  • Aegis HaloBell Pro 2K: High-definition smart video doorbell with edge motion intelligence, two-way audio, and local MicroSD/NAS storage.
  • Aegis AeroFlow Ultra BLDC Fan: Energy-efficient smart ceiling fan featuring WhisperQuiet™ brushless DC motor technology and 10 digital speed settings.
  • Aegis FrostSense Inverter Hub: Smart dual-inverter climate control unit with real-time temperature, humidity, and laser air quality sensing.
  • Aegis LuminaGlow: Tunable RGB+CCT smart mesh bulb offering 16 million colors and natural circadian rhythm lighting.
  • Aegis BreezeTower Pro: Smart bladeless oscillating fan equipped with True HEPA H13 filtration.
  • Aegis ChimeLink Hub: Wireless multi-room smart doorbell chime and range extender.

Our Core Principles

1. Direct Wi-Fi Connectivity: Zero proprietary bridges or vendor-locked hubs required. All devices connect directly to standard 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz routers.

2. Local-First Operation: Your home automations, wall schedules, and temperature regulators continue functioning without interruption even if external internet connectivity drops.

3. Transparent Pricing & Zero Traps: We provide full hardware ownership without holding essential features hostage behind mandatory cloud subscription plans.
