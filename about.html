Who We Are

AEGIS IoT Technologies is a specialized Smart Home Automation Service Provider and IoT Systems Integration firm. We bridge the gap between complex connected hardware and seamless, comfortable living.

Our Core Service Deliverables

We provide end-to-end turnkey home automation services for residential flats, standalone villas, bungalows, and commercial workspaces:

  • Smart Home Consultation & Site Audits: Comprehensive on-site evaluations of electrical wiring, neutral wire presence, switchboard depths, and Wi-Fi radio coverage.
  • Turnkey Hardware Procurement: Supplying pre-tested, generic Wi-Fi smart devices including smart video doorbells, silent BLDC fans, dual-inverter AC controllers, and tunable ambient lighting without retail markups.
  • Certified Electrical Installation: Safe, physical wiring execution by certified electricians, ensuring zero visible wires and seamless concealed switchboard retrofits.
  • Custom Automation Programming: Configuring automated daily scenes (e.g., "Good Morning", "Night Setback Cooling", "Away Mode Geofencing", "Vacation Security Routine").
  • Ecosystem Integration: Unifying all devices across Apple HomeKit, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, Matter, and Home Assistant into one intuitive control surface.
  • Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC): Ongoing preventive maintenance visits, Wi-Fi mesh optimization, routine firmware upgrades, and priority on-site technician support.
