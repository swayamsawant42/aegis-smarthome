Our Core Services

End-to-End Smart Automation Services

From initial blueprint consultation to certified on-site installation and lifetime maintenance, AEGIS handles every dimension of your connected home.

1. Site Audit & System Design

Our IoT engineers evaluate your home architecture, existing switchboard wiring, and Wi-Fi signal coverage to engineer a customized, reliable automation blueprint.

2. Certified On-Site Installation

Licensed electricians and smart systems technicians complete all physical wiring, retrofit switches, pair voice assistants, and program automated time-of-day scenes.

3. AMC & Preventive Maintenance

Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) include quarterly health checks, Wi-Fi mesh optimization, priority emergency dispatch, and free component replacement.

4. Energy Setback Optimization

We program algorithmic temperature setbacks on your ACs and schedules on high-draw appliances to lower monthly power bills by up to 35%.

5. Multi-User Family Training

We train all family members on using intuitive voice commands, custom smartphone widgets, and emergency overrides so everyone enjoys smart living effortlessly.

Open Standards

Our Service Philosophy: No Walled Gardens

Unlike brand manufacturers who lock you into their closed ecosystems and proprietary hubs, AEGIS works for you—integrating reliable generic Wi-Fi hardware with open standards and zero mandatory cloud subscriptions.

Direct Wi-Fi 6 & Dual-Band

No expensive proprietary gateway. Devices link directly to any standard 2.4 GHz or 5 GHz home Wi-Fi network with ultra-low latency and OFDMA spectrum efficiency.

Local-First Network Control

Internet outage? Automations and wall-switches continue to function without interruption over your local area network (LAN) using Matter over Wi-Fi.

WPA3 & Zero Cloud Lock

End-to-end hardware encryption (AES-128/256), secure boot microcontrollers, and zero mandatory cloud recording fees. Your video feeds and sensor logs belong to you.
