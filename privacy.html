Privacy Policy

Your privacy is foundational to AEGIS. We design local-first hardware that puts you in control of your data.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: November 16, 2026
● Data Protected

1. Overview & Commitment

At AEGIS IoT Technologies ("AEGIS", "we", "our", or "us"), we are deeply committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of visitors to our website (swayamsawant42.github.io/aegis-smarthome/) and customers who utilize our smart appliances.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal and technical information we collect, how it is processed and protected, and your rights under the Information Technology Act, 2000, and applicable global data protection standards.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact & Order Information: When you submit an inquiry, place an order, or register for support, we may collect your name, email address (aegisapod@gmail.com), contact phone number (7410190640), shipping address, and GST identification details.
  • Payment Information: For purchases made on our platform, all payment transactions are routed securely through authorized payment gateway partners, including Razorpay. We do NOT collect, store, or have access to your full credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or online banking passwords.
  • Device & Network Telemetry: When interacting with our simulator or local IoT APIs, anonymous diagnostic logs (such as Wi-Fi signal RSSI, firmware version, and device uptime) are processed locally to facilitate network diagnostics.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely for:

  1. Scheduling on-site electrical feasibility audits and technician installation appointments.
  2. Processing, provisioning, and dispatching smart hardware modules included in your automation packages.
  3. Providing ongoing Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) support, firmware updates, and honoring service warranties.
  4. Sending electronic service receipts, appointment confirmation messages, and diagnostic reports.
  5. Preventing fraudulent transactions and ensuring network security.

4. Payment Security & Third-Party Gateways

Razorpay PCI DSS Compliance: All online payments are handled directly by Razorpay Software Private Limited. Razorpay is certified PCI DSS Level 1 compliant, which is the highest international standard of security in the payments industry. Transmission of payment data is encrypted using 256-bit Secure Socket Layer (SSL) technology.

5. Smart Device Privacy (Local-First Guarantee)

Unlike conventional smart home systems that upload live video feeds and microphone recordings to third-party public clouds, AEGIS appliances are engineered with a Local-First Architecture:

  • HaloBell Pro 2K Video: Real-time video feeds and motion recordings are stored directly on the on-device MicroSD card or your private Network Attached Storage (NAS) via RTSP/ONVIF. Video feeds are never stored or accessed by AEGIS staff.
  • Environmental Sensors: Temperature, humidity, and fan RPM schedules run entirely on your local Wi-Fi router.

6. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses standard essential cookies and local storage (`localStorage`) strictly to remember your interface preferences (such as Dark/Light theme mode). We do not employ invasive cross-site advertising tracking cookies.

7. Data Retention & User Rights

You have the right to request a copy of your personal data, rectify inaccuracies, or request the deletion of your customer profile from our internal fulfillment database by writing to our designated email at aegisapod@gmail.com.
