Refunds, Returns & Cancellation Policy

Transparent, fair, and hassle-free return and cancellation terms for all AEGIS smart home devices.

Applicable to: All Online Transactions & Hardware Purchases
● 7-Day Return Policy
Key Highlights at a Glance:
• Order Cancellation: 100% full refund before shipment dispatch.
• Return Window: 7 days from the date of product delivery.
• Refund Processing: Approved refunds are credited back to the original source account / payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
• Shipping Timelines: Dispatch in 24–48 hours; delivery in 3–5 working days nationwide.

1. Order Cancellation Policy

We understand that requirements can change. Customers have the right to cancel their orders under the following conditions:

  • Cancellation Before Dispatch: You can cancel any order free of charge at any time prior to the package being dispatched from our fulfillment warehouse. Upon cancellation, a 100% full refund will be initiated immediately.
  • Cancellation After Dispatch: If the order has already been handed over to our courier logistics partner, it cannot be intercepted in transit. You may refuse delivery upon arrival, and once the package returns to our warehouse in unopened condition, your refund will be processed minus nominal return courier charges.
  • How to Cancel: To cancel an order, email us at aegisapod@gmail.com with your Order ID or call our helpline at +91 7410190640.

2. 7-Day Return & Replacement Policy

All AEGIS IoT smart products (including smart doorbells, BLDC ceiling fans, inverter AC hubs, and lights) are covered by our 7-Day Replacement & Return Window starting from the day of delivery.

Eligible Reasons for Return:

  • Product delivered in a physically damaged or defective state.
  • Missing parts, accessories, or mismatched hardware model.
  • Technical hardware malfunction occurring within 7 days of installation.

Conditions for Valid Return:

  • The product must be in its original packaging including all user manuals, mounting brackets, screws, and cables.
  • The serial number / barcode on the unit must be intact and match invoice records.
  • Items damaged through improper electrical wiring, power surges beyond 240V, or unauthorized physical tampering are not eligible for standard returns (covered under warranty repair terms).

Once our warehouse team receives and inspects the returned hardware (usually within 24 to 48 hours of return receipt):

4. Shipping & Delivery Policy

  • Processing Time: All confirmed orders are verified and packed within 24 to 48 business hours.
  • Delivery Transit Time: We ship across all serviceable pin codes in India via standard surface and air express couriers (Delhivery, BlueDart, DTDC). Expected delivery timeline is 3 to 5 business days from dispatch date.
  • Shipping Charges: We provide Free Shipping on all domestic prepaid orders above ₹1,999. For orders below this value, a flat shipping fee of ₹99 applies.
  • Tracking: An automated dispatch email and SMS containing the courier AWB tracking number is shared as soon as the package leaves our warehouse.

5. 1-Year Comprehensive Warranty

Beyond the 7-day return window, all AEGIS smart devices are protected under a 1-Year Limited Hardware Warranty. In the rare event of a hardware breakdown, our technical support engineers will arrange free repair or complete unit replacement.
