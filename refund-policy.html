Services Cancellation, Rescheduling & Refund Policy

Fair, transparent cancellation guidelines for smart home installation bookings, consultation appointments, and AMC service plans.

Applicable to: All Smart Home Service Bookings, Turnkey Packages & AMC Plans
● Verified Policy
Key Highlights at a Glance:
• Service Appointment Cancellation: 100% full refund if cancelled up to 24 hours prior to technician site visit.
• Appointment Rescheduling: Free of charge up to 12 hours before scheduled slot.
• Hardware Component Returns: 7 days replacement window for any hardware module installed during service.
• Refund Processing: Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original source account / payment method within 5 to 7 business days via Razorpay.

1. Service Appointment Cancellation

Clients may cancel an automation consultation, electrical feasibility audit, or installation booking subject to the following timelines:

  • Cancellation 24+ Hours Before Visit: If you cancel your scheduled appointment at least 24 hours in advance, you are entitled to an immediate 100% full refund of all service fees and advance deposits paid.
  • Cancellation Within 24 Hours: If a cancellation is requested less than 24 hours prior to the confirmed technician slot (where staff and transit logistics have already been allocated), a nominal rescheduling/cancellation fee of ₹499 will be deducted from the refund.
  • How to Cancel: Simply email your Booking ID to aegisapod@gmail.com or call/WhatsApp our support desk at +91 7410190640.

2. Appointment Rescheduling

We accommodate unforeseen schedule shifts. You may reschedule any installation or audit appointment free of charge by providing at least 12 hours notice prior to the original appointment time. We will re-allocate our certified technicians to your newly requested preferred date and time slot.

3. 7-Day Installed Hardware Replacement Window

All smart devices provided and integrated under our turnkey automation packages (including smart video doorbells, BLDC ceiling fans, inverter AC hubs, and lights) are backed by our 7-Day Replacement Guarantee starting from the installation completion date.

If any hardware component demonstrates physical defects, Wi-Fi pairing instability, or component failure within 7 days of installation, our technicians will visit your premises and provide an immediate on-site replacement free of charge.

4. Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) Cancellation

Clients enrolled in our Standard or VIP Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) may terminate their contract at any point during the term. Refunds for cancelled AMC contracts will be calculated on a pro-rata basis for the remaining unserviced quarters of the year, minus standard administrative processing charges.

5. Refund Method & Processing Timelines

Once a cancellation or return is verified by our customer care team:

Payment Method Used Refund Destination Turnaround Time
UPI (Google Pay / PhonePe / Paytm) Original Linked Bank Account 2 to 4 Business Days
Credit Card / Debit Card Original Issuing Bank Card 5 to 7 Business Days
Net Banking Source Bank Account 3 to 5 Business Days
Razorpay Wallet / PayLater Original Source Balance 24 to 48 Hours

*Important: All refunds are credited exclusively back to the original payment method used during checkout through our authorized payment aggregator Razorpay. We do not issue cash refunds or hand third-party bearer cheques.

6. Service Delivery & Execution Timelines

  • Consultation & Site Audits: Scheduled within 24 to 48 hours of booking confirmation based on customer slot selection.
  • Turnkey Installation Execution: Standard 1-2 BHK installations are completed within 1 business day; large villa projects are executed in phased milestones (typically 2 to 4 days) in coordination with site managers.
  • Emergency AMC Support: Priority on-site technician response within 24 hours for Standard AMC, or within 4 hours for VIP Priority AMC subscribers.
