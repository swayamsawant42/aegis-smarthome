1. Scope of Services

These Terms and Conditions ("Terms") govern all smart home automation services provided by AEGIS IoT Technologies ("AEGIS", "Service Provider", "We", or "Us") to the customer ("Client", "You").

Our services encompass: (a) on-site electrical feasibility audits and IoT blueprint consulting; (b) procurement and bench-testing of generic Wi-Fi smart appliances; (c) certified on-site electrical installation, switch retrofitting, and Wi-Fi mesh optimization; (d) automated routine programming and voice-assistant pairing; and (e) Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) and on-demand technical support.

2. Service Appointments & Site Access

  • Site Access: The Client agrees to grant AEGIS certified technicians reasonable access to the premises, electrical distribution panels, and Wi-Fi networking equipment at the agreed appointment time.

  • Appointment Rescheduling: The Client may reschedule an installation or audit appointment free of charge by notifying us at least 12 hours prior to the scheduled visit.
